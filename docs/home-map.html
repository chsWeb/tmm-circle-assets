How the top section works

Section 1 is different from the rest. Sections 2 to 5 each show the newest posts from one space. Section 1 looks across several spaces and shows only posts tagged featured. If nothing is tagged, the section disappears rather than showing an empty box.

Sections with nothing to show are hidden too, which is why some levels have shorter pages than others. Those rows sit on a grey background here, with a note saying why.

This map shows the layout for each level, not one member's page. On top of everything here, the home screen asks Circle which spaces the signed-in member is actually in and refuses to show a section whose space is not on that list. So a member only ever sees the rows they can open, even if their level was matched wrongly.
